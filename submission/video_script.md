# Kervan Credit Passport — demo video script (3:30 target, hard cap 4:00)

Record the screen at 1080p with the app on the left two-thirds and your webcam or voice only. Use a test business you prepared beforehand (for example the cashmere workshop fixture files in `fixtures/personas/persona_a/`) so the run takes about 30 seconds live. Run it once before recording so the model calls are warm.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Title slide or the app header | "Kervan Credit Passport. In Mongolia most small businesses keep their books in a notebook, a bank account and a QR payment app. Banks can't underwrite that, so they don't lend. Kervan turns those informal records into a verified, explainable credit passport." |
| 0:20–0:45 | Business view, loan request form | "The owner fills in what they declare: revenue, years in business, the amount and tenor. Then they drop their records: a photo of the handwritten ledger in Mongolian, the bank statement, and a payment export. Nothing is typed from the documents." |
| 0:45–1:30 | Click Run assessment; pipeline panel runs | "Now the agent runs live. Read: Gemini reads each document with structured output and returns every figure with a page or row pointer. Verify: Python re-sums the CSVs and checks continuity and ratios; Gemini adds qualitative checks. Notice the warning: declared revenue is 45 percent above the bank inflows. Score: a deterministic scorecard, no LLM, that's our core principle. Explain and Write: Gemini turns the result into words." |
| 1:30–2:10 | Passport card: grade, PD, factor bars, flags, plan | "Here is the passport. Grade K3 with points, PD estimate and rate band. Each factor bar shows its verification status: solid is verified, dashed is self-declared and counts half, hatched is inconsistent and counts zero. The improvement plan shows what the owner can do, and every expected gain was computed by re-running the scorecard, not guessed by the model. Toggle to Mongolian for the owner's explanation." |
| 2:10–2:50 | Bank view: list, memo, make offer; back to Business to accept | "The bank sees the passport anonymized: sector, request and verified numbers only. Gemini drafted the credit memo: cash-flow analysis, risks, mitigants, the scorecard table and an evidence appendix. The bank makes an offer; the business accepts, and only then is the identity revealed." |
| 2:50–3:20 | Agent trust view: guardrails, trace, evidence trail | "Every claim is auditable. The step trace shows prompt versions, model, latency and schema validation. The evidence trail links every figure to its document and row with a confidence. Guardrails: the model cannot change the score, invented numbers are blocked, humans approve edge cases, no protected attributes." |
| 3:20–3:40 | Architecture slide | "Under the hood: FastAPI on Cloud Run, Gemini through the google-genai SDK with structured output, Secret Manager for keys, Cloud Build for deploys. Same pipeline, new scorecard config and language notes, and it scales across Central Asia. Thank you." |

Tips: keep the mouse still while narrating, say the grade and the flag out loud when they appear, and cut any pause longer than two seconds in editing.
